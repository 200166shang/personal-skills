# Personal Skills

This glossary defines the terms shared by this collection. Learning workflows and media-note production are separate areas; similarly named artifacts are interpreted within their section.

## Learning Workflows

**Root Question**:
An exploratory entry point for learning an unfamiliar area; it can lead to recursive Questions as understanding develops.
_Avoid_: Interview prompt

**Topic**:
A coherent, readable synthesis of what the learner has explored about a subject. It aims to preserve the full explanation rather than select only interview material.
_Avoid_: Interview answer

**Review**:
A practice session where the learner explains a previously learned module's solution or implementation, and receives feedback on the most important gaps plus a clearer spoken version. It aims for understandable explanation, not exhaustive recall of saved details.
_Avoid_: Detail quiz, comprehensive question bank

**Review Scope**:
One previously studied Topic or project module explicitly selected for a Review session. Related Questions, Research, and implementation material can support it; neighboring modules do not become separate review subjects.

**Interview Q&A**:
A finalized, reusable, module-representative interview prompt—proposed by the assistant or specified by the learner—paired with a brief reason it is worth practicing and a strong spoken answer. It invites a coherent explanation of the solution or implementation rather than testing an isolated saved detail; the learner's practice attempts are separate.
_Avoid_: Practice transcript, attempt history

**Spoken Reference Answer**:
A natural interview response that starts with plain language, keeps the technical terms needed for accuracy, and connects the solution to supported implementation evidence. It should be explainable aloud in roughly one to three minutes, not read like a textbook entry.

## Skills and Installed Copies

**Skill**:
A reusable capability with instructions and supporting resources, which a calling agent can use independently or combine with other capabilities.

**Installed Copy**:
A deployed copy of a Skill for a local host; the maintained source remains in this collection.

**Calling Agent**:
The user's agent that selects and combines Skills for a request. Its identity is independent of the media capabilities it uses.

## Media Notes

**Lesson**:
One video and its matching timestamped transcript, treated as one source scope for an illustrated lecture script.
_Avoid_: Whole course when referring to one part

**Illustrated Lecture Script**:
A readable account of a lesson supported by selected source-video screenshots; its content mode determines the permitted degree of condensation.

**Faithful Mode**:
A lecture script that retains the speaker's substantive expression, order, examples and qualifications while cleaning speech and clear recognition errors.
_Avoid_: Summary as the default meaning of notes

**Summary Mode**:
A deliberately condensed version of a lesson requested explicitly by the user.

**Batch**:
A fixed selection of prepared local lessons whose independent note-production tasks are managed together.
_Avoid_: End-to-end download/transcription workflow

**Batch Coordinator**:
The calling agent's role that dispatches lesson tasks and reconciles progress and results.

**Lesson Attempt**:
One assigned execution of writing or reviewing a particular lesson; its outcome belongs only to that assignment.

**Checkpoint**:
Verified progress saved so an unfinished lesson can continue later; it is not a completed script.

**Independent Lesson Review**:
A separate review of one lesson's complete source and final illustrations, distinct from the Learning Review practice session.
_Avoid_: Structural validation as a synonym for content review
